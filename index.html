ABOUT ME

I'm Kamsi, a designer working across graphic design, motion and video. I help brands, projects and ideas find their visual voice — whether that's a logo system, a sixty-second reel or a campaign that actually makes people stop scrolling.

What started as a love for typography and layout slowly grew into something bigger. I got curious about movement, then about time-based storytelling, and before I knew it I was designing identities that breathe, motion pieces that feel alive and edits that carry rhythm. The work kept pulling me into new territory and I followed it.

Everything I make starts with a concept. I'm not interested in decoration for its own sake. I want the work to mean something, feel considered and hold up under a second look. I obsess over the details most people won't consciously notice — because those are the details that make something feel right.

What sets the work apart is that it doesn't sit still. It shifts between disciplines without losing a clear point of view. That's intentional. The best visual work doesn't care about categories. It just needs to connect.
