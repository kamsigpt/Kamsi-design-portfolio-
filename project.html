FORGE

Graphic Design · Motion 2024 Art Direction, Design, Motion

THE CHALLENGE

FORGE came from a conversation about what it means to build something that lasts. The brand needed a visual identity that felt solid and considered — something that communicated craftsmanship without leaning on clichés. The challenge was making "strength" feel modern and approachable, not heavy-handed. We needed a system that could work across print, digital and motion while keeping a consistent voice.

THE CREATIVE DIRECTION

The direction centred on controlled contrast — pairing sharp geometric forms with moments of breathing room. We built the palette around deep industrial tones offset by a single warm accent that gives the whole system a pulse. Typography was chosen for its clarity and presence at every scale. Every element was designed to feel intentional, like nothing was placed by accident.

THE EXECUTION

The identity rolled out across a suite of brand materials — business systems, social templates, environmental graphics and a motion toolkit. Each piece was built to stand on its own but feel like part of the same conversation when seen together. The motion work focused on transitions that mirror the brand's ethos: clean entries, precise movement and finishes that land with weight.

We also developed a set of animation principles for the brand — rules for timing, easing and layering that keep every motion piece feeling consistent regardless of who's animating or what the context is.

THE RESULT

The final identity gave FORGE a visual presence that's unmistakable. It's a system that scales without losing its character — from a social post to a billboard, from a static page to a sixty-second reel. The client walked away with not just a logo but a complete visual language they could use, extend and grow with. More importantly, it feels like something that will still hold up two years from now.
